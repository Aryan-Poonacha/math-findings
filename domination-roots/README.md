# Integer domination roots: −10, and small graphs with root −4

Paper: [paper.pdf](paper.pdf) (source `paper.tex`; numbers in `tables.tex` are generated by `verify.py`).

New results (D(G,x) = sum of x^|S| over dominating sets S):
1. A connected 373-vertex, 673-edge graph G10 (a hub with 22 leaves and 45 attached blocks) has the simple root −10. With known results, the integers r in [−11,−1] with D(G,r) = 0 for some graph are exactly −2, −4, −6, −8, −10.
2. No graph on at most 12 vertices has domination root −4 (exhaustive search), so the least order is 13, 14 or 15.
3. An explicit connected 16-vertex graph with a verified simple root −4. MathArena already reported an accepted 16-vertex witness but published only its 15-vertex edge list; with its 15-vertex graph and its support joins with stars, that gives connected graphs with root −4 of every order n ≥ 15. The paper writes this out with a simple root at every order.
4. Roots −4, −6, −8, −10 in one connected graph with any prescribed multiplicities: MathArena's join covers −4, −6, −8 and Ma–Zhang −4 alone; −10 is the new ingredient.

Known before (cited in the paper): odd integers are never roots (Brouwer–Csorba–Schrijver 2009); the root −4 (Alikhani–Griswold 2026); graphs with roots −6, −8, the 15-vertex graph with root −4, a reported 16-vertex witness, support joins, the block-ratio search method and 12 ≤ n_min(−4) ≤ 15 (MathArena, October 2026); a 21-vertex graph and a family of orders 33r+2 with root −4 (Omega Institute, Lean); a 30-vertex graph (Reshetnikov); every order ≥ 35 and multiplicity r for −4 (Ma–Zhang); the joining lemma (Kotek et al. 2012).

Check: default mode checks the explicit witnesses and tables, enumerates connected graphs through order 7, and excludes −4 at order 8. Full mode certifies the exclusion through order 12.
- `python3 -B verify.py`: standard library only, about 1 s.
- `python3 -B verify.py --full NAUTY_DIR` (NAUTY_DIR = a built nauty 2.8.9 source tree): needs gcc, about 1 h on 15 threads. It resumes from `rerun/`, reusing a slice only when the binary's sha256, the slice arguments and a complete result record match. Logs, build commands and hashes: `logs/`.

Data: `data/graphs.json` (G10 blocks, G16, MathArena's G4, G6, G8), `data/G10-edges.txt`.
